Back to the Mirror

Two questionnaires: the OODA interview, on how you observe, orient, decide and act, and Get to Know You!, on what you hold sacred, the pictures you carry of yourself, your feelings, what you think doesn't matter, and what you count as good and bad. Answer either or both, in any order. Every answer is saved as you go, so you can stop and come back. All your answers become one corpus, and building it makes a Mirror of you.
